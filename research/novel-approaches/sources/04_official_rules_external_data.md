## Verbatim quotes
> "You may use data other than the Competition Data (“External Data”) to develop and test your Submissions."
> "you will ensure the External Data is either publicly available and equally accessible to use by all Participants of the Competition for purposes of the competition at no cost to the other Participants, or satisfies the Reasonableness criteria"
> "Purchasing a license to use a proprietary dataset that exceeds the cost of a prize in the competition would not be considered reasonable."
> "You may submit a maximum of five (5) Submissions per day."
## What it supports
- FACT: external data and models are allowed, subject to public availability or the reasonableness standard. One prize = $5,000.
- FACT (combined with host comments in sources/20): self-generated data within a reasonable cost (host: token spend beyond about $5k is unreasonable) need not be public.
